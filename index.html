The Essence of NAQSH

An imprint, not an announcement.

In Urdu and Arabic, naqsh means an engraving — a mark pressed into something and left there. That is the idea we build every fragrance around. Not a scent that arrives loudly and fades, but one that settles into memory the way a carved line settles into stone.

We don't make perfumes to be noticed. We make them to be remembered.

Our Story

Founded on a single idea.

NAQSH began with a question: why does a fragrance have to disappear the moment you stop thinking about it? We wanted to build scents that behave more like memory — returning at unexpected moments, attached to a person rather than a product.

What started as a small atelier experimenting with unconventional accords has become a house with a defined language: restraint over spectacle, character over trend, and every formula built to be worn for years rather than a single season.

Every bottle that leaves NAQSH carries that same intention — to be a mark someone else remembers you by.
